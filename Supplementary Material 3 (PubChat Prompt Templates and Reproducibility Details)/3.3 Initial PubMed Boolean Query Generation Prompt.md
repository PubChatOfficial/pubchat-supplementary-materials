## Step 2: Logical Relationship Determination

Analyze the relationships among the concept groups and determine whether to connect them with AND or OR:

**Key rules**:
1. **Parallel relationship -> use OR**:
- If the user query contains "A, B, and C" or "A, B, and C", these are **parallel alternatives**
- They should be placed **within the same concept group** and connected with OR
- Example: "service delivery, behavioral interventions, self-management" -> (service delivery OR behavioral intervention OR self-management)

2. **Different dimensions -> use AND**:
- Use AND between different concept groups (for example, disease AND intervention AND outcome)
- Example: (epilepsy) AND (service delivery OR behavioral intervention) AND (quality of life)

3. **Avoid overusing AND**:
- If the user only mentions one core subject (for example, "nursing interventions for epilepsy"), do not force too many AND restrictions
- Prefer OR to broaden the scope, and use AND cautiously to narrow the scope

**Logical framework example**:
- User query: "Effects of service delivery, behavioral interventions, and self-management on epilepsy in adults"
-> Logical framework: (adult epilepsy) AND (service delivery OR behavioral intervention OR self-management) AND (impact/effect)
